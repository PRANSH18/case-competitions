# Case Competitions

Work from national case and analytics competitions during the PGDM at Great Lakes Institute of Management, Chennai.

## WWT Unravel 2025: recommendation system for Wings R Us

**The brief.** Recommend the right last-minute items at checkout to lift average order value and retention for a US chicken-wings chain, measured on Recall@3.

**The solution.** A personalised recommendation pipeline built on order, customer and store data. It combines best-selling item combinations, customer purchase history and store context to suggest three add-on items at checkout. The deck is explicit about its assumptions and its limit with first-time customers.

## Crack-A-Case: Zepto product teardown

A product manager's view of everyday friction in the Zepto app: an overwhelming interface and excessive ads, weak support and content controls, and a hard-to-find search bar with no direct way to remove items from the cart. It sets the usability findings against Zepto's business model and finances, and recommends fixes.

[Presentation (PDF)](zepto-crack-a-case/Zepto_Product_Teardown.pdf)

## L&T Finance Challenge (Trilytics '25): farmer income prediction

A model to predict farmer income from demographic, location, land-ownership and non-farm income features, evaluated on mean absolute percentage error. It uses geographic clustering, encoding of banded variables and a stacked ensemble.

[farmer.py](lt-finance-farmer-income/farmer.py) · [feature.py](lt-finance-farmer-income/feature.py)

The challenge dataset belongs to L&T Finance and is not included.
